Remote Nominee Services Opportunity

We provide compliant nominee director and verification support services to international companies navigating global regulatory requirements. Our platform connects qualified individuals with businesses seeking assistance in jurisdictions where nominee arrangements are legally permitted, such as for privacy and operational efficiency.

All services adhere to international standards, including anti-money laundering (AML) regulations and know-your-customer (KYC) protocols. Participants are fully informed of their legal responsibilities as nominee directors, which may include limited administrative duties with no operational control.

This is a remote, contract-based opportunity for responsible professionals seeking supplemental income through verified, legal processes.

What You'll Provide

International businesses frequently require nominee support for company formations and verifications in specific countries. As a contractor, you may assist with:

Nominee Director Services

Acting as a nominee director for registration purposes, with full disclosure of your limited role and responsibilities.

Identity Verification (KYC)

Providing verified identity documentation for standard know-your-customer checks required during company onboarding.

Administrative Filings

Supporting administrative filings for company onboarding processes across various jurisdictions.

Remote Compliance Coordination

Remote coordination for compliance-related tasks, all from the comfort of your own location.

Important: All engagements are contract-based, with clear terms outlined in advance. You retain the right to review and sign off on all documents, and we ensure compatibility with your local laws.

No Prior Experience Required, But Compliance Matters

While no specific corporate experience is needed, you must meet the following criteria:

  • Age & Residency Be at least 21 years old and a resident of an eligible country
  • Valid Identification Have valid government-issued ID and proof of address
  • Legal Awareness Understand basic legal obligations (we provide training materials)
  • Ethical Standards Commit to ethical standards and full transparency

Guided Onboarding

We guide you through onboarding, including a compliance checklist and legal overview. Our dedicated support team ensures you are fully prepared before any engagement begins.

Training Materials

Comprehensive training documentation is provided to help you understand your role, legal responsibilities, and best practices for nominee services.
